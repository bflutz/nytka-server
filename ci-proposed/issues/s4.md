# Retention: default to 3650 days for raw audio (stands in for 'keep indefinitely')

Phase 1 ([plan](https://github.com/bflutz/nytka-server/blob/phase1/docs/voice-recorder-plan.md), R9). Barend wants raw audio kept indefinitely. The 10-year setting stands in for that. `Nytka__Audio__RetentionDays` defaults to 14 (range 0-3650, configuration.md line 41).

**Change:**
- Set the default to 3650 in code, `docker-compose.yml` and the docs. Leave the range as it is.
- Transcripts, memories and derived data are unaffected, as today.

**Done when:**
- A test shows a fresh install keeps audio 3650 days and an explicit env value still overrides it.
- configuration.md and operations.md (storage estimate of ~18 MB per hour of speech, ~53 GB a year at 8 h of speech a day) say so.
