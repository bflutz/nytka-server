# Backlog: retry waiting transcriptions automatically whenever BMain is reachable

Phase 1 ([plan](https://github.com/bflutz/nytka-server/blob/phase1/docs/voice-recorder-plan.md), §4 'BMain off'). Barend decided BMain is not woken automatically: the backlog should drain whenever BMain happens to be on. Today, after 3 tries and backoff of +1/2/4/8/16 h, failed batches wait for a manual `POST /api/v1/transcriptions/retry` (configuration.md).

**Change:**
- Add a background job that probes the STT endpoint every `Nytka__Stt__ProbeMinutes` minutes (default 5). The probe should be cheap: `GET /health` or `/v1/models` on the same host, with a 3 s timeout.
- When the probe turns from down to up, or is up while batches are waiting, re-queue every batch that failed or is waiting for STT, exactly as the retry endpoint does. Keep the existing backoff for real errors, such as a 4xx answer or bad audio.
- While STT is down, don't count the failures toward the batch's give-up rounds.
- Show the backlog in `/api/v1/info` or the status endpoint: the waiting count, the oldest waiting capture time and the last time STT was reachable.

**Done when:**
- Test: with STT down, uploads queue and none is given up after 48 h of simulated time. When STT comes up, all of them are transcribed in one pass, in capture order.
- The audio of a waiting batch is never deleted by retention while it is still untranscribed.
- The settings are documented and the build is clean.
